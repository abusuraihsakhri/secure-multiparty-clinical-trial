Clinical Trial Aggregate Survival

Local Kaplan–Meier and log-rank demonstration · educational use

Source repository ↗
Synthetic or approved aggregate data only. This browser app does not upload inputs, implement garbled circuits, perform secret sharing, or provide cryptographically secure multiparty computation. Aggregate cells may still disclose sensitive information. Do not enter patient-level data or protected health information. Results are not clinical recommendations.

1 · Multicenter aggregate event counts

JSON records: site, arm (control/treatment), initial participants n, and time-binned events/censored. Events are assumed to precede censoring at the same recorded time.

Distinct site/arm combinations required; participants cannot be removed more than once. Both arms required.
